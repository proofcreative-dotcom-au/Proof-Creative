CASE STUDY – BRAND IDENTITY

DMS Surveying & Spatial Services

A complete brand identity for a Brisbane surveying practice – precise, confident and built to work everywhere from site signage to spatial reports.

CLIENT
DMS Surveying & Spatial Services
ROLE
Brand Identity
DELIVERABLES
Logo, identity system, brand guidelines
SECTOR
Professional services

The Challenge

DMS needed an identity that read as established and precise from day one – trustworthy to developers and builders, credible alongside long-standing competitors, and practical enough to apply across vehicles, site signage, drawings and reports without a design team on hand.

The Process

The identity was built around the language of the trade itself – measured lines, fixed points and clear coordinates – resolved into a mark and system that hold up at every scale, from a survey peg to a vehicle wrap. A tight palette and typographic system keep every application consistent without specialist tools.

The Result

A confident, working identity that positions DMS as an established practice – consistent across every touchpoint and simple enough for the team to apply themselves, from proposal documents to the site gate.
