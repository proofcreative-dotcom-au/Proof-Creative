Packaging Design & Realisation

From packaging concept to final artwork, Proof Creative turns ideas into accurate, consistent and production-ready systems. These four capabilities run in sequence – most projects enter at one of them and carry through to press.

CAPABILITIES
01

Packaging Design

Packaging Design is creating the packaging concept and the design system that holds it together – structure, hierarchy, typography, colour and graphic language for the pack itself.

THIS IS NOT

This is not artwork production. This is the creative decision-making that everything downstream is built from.

YOU SUPPLY
Brief, product details, market context and any existing brand assets.
YOU GET BACK
Concept routes, a resolved pack design, and the design system rules behind it.
02

Design Realisation

Design Realisation is taking an approved creative direction and building it into accurate, production-ready artwork – resolved across every product, format and production requirement it has to survive.

THIS IS NOT

This is not redesigning the creative, and it is not a PDF export. The idea is already signed off – this is the technical build that makes it hold up across a real range and run correctly on press.

YOU SUPPLY
Approved design, dielines, SKU list, formats, copy, barcodes, regulatory text and supplier information.
YOU GET BACK
Print-ready artwork built to specification and versioned, with the system resolved across every SKU and format.
03

3D / Visualisation

3D and Visualisation is producing realistic packaging and product imagery before anything is manufactured.

THIS IS NOT

This is not a mock-up for a mood board. These are accurate renders built from real dielines and specified materials, used for approval, investment and pre-launch sales.

YOU SUPPLY
Dielines or structural drawings, artwork, and material and finish intent.
YOU GET BACK
Photoreal renders and animation for approvals, pitches, listings and pre-launch marketing.
04

Print & Production Management

Print and Production Management is specifying how the pack is actually made, then checking, proofing and liaising with suppliers until it runs correctly on press.

THIS IS NOT

This is not sending files and hoping. It is specification, pre-production checking, and being on the phone to the printer.

ADDITIONAL SCOPE

Attending press checks in person where a job warrants it – passing sheets, approving colour on press and signing off first-off samples before the run continues.

YOU SUPPLY
Approved artwork, supplier details, timings and commercial constraints.
YOU GET BACK
Print specifications, embellishment and material direction, checked proofs, and supplier liaison through to press.
