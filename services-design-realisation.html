01 / 03
01

Packaging design

We develop the concept and visual system for your packaging: structure, information hierarchy, typography, colour and graphic language. The aim is a resolved pack design, with clear principles to guide its wider application.

Starting point
Brief, product details, market context and any existing brand assets.
What we deliver
Concept routes, a resolved pack design, and the design system rules behind it.
Scope

Concept development and pack design form this stage. Production artwork can be scoped as the next step.

02

Design realisation

An approved design still needs careful work before it reaches production. We build the artwork across products, sizes and formats, resolving the technical requirements while preserving the creative direction.

Starting point
Approved design, dielines, SKU list, formats, copy, barcodes, regulatory text and supplier information.
What we deliver
Print-ready artwork built to specification and versioned, with the system resolved across every SKU and format.
Scope

This stage carries an approved direction into production. Changes to the creative direction are agreed separately.

03

3D visualisation

See the packaging before it is made. Working from structural drawings, artwork and specified materials, we create realistic renders and animation for design approvals, presentations and pre-launch marketing.

Starting point
Dielines or structural drawings, artwork, and material and finish intent.
What we deliver
Photoreal renders and animation for approvals, pitches, listings and pre-launch marketing.
04

Print & production management

The finished pack depends on more than the artwork. We develop specifications, review proofs and work with suppliers to resolve materials, colour and finishes through production. Where required, in-person press checks can be scoped separately.

Starting point
Approved artwork, supplier details, timings and commercial constraints.
What we deliver
Print specifications, embellishment and material direction, checked proofs, and supplier liaison through to press.
On-site support

Where agreed, attending press checks in person: passing sheets, reviewing colour on press and signing off first-off samples before the run continues.

Specialist structural engineering, photography, printing and other third-party production services are scoped separately when required. We coordinate with approved specialists rather than implying every discipline is performed in-house.
