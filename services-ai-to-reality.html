We scope the stages your concept needs, from an initial assessment through to its intended application.

The image is a starting point.

The next step is to resolve the decisions behind it: logo construction, typography, colour systems, dielines, materials and finishes. Each needs to work as part of a consistent design.

We assess what can be carried forward, redraw what needs development and prepare the assets for the way they will be used.
