Proof Creative is the bridge between steps one and four. Most studios work at either end.

Generating a concept is now the easy part.

AI has made concept creation fast and cheap. What it has not made easier is everything that comes next: making a concept consistent, reproducible, ownable and physically manufacturable. A generated image is a picture of a brand. It is not a brand, and it is not artwork.

That conversion is the work, and it needs someone who understands logo construction, type, colour systems, dielines, substrates and how a press actually behaves. Proof Creative rebuilds generated concepts so they survive contact with production.
