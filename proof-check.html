06 / A few useful answers

Scope, timing & next steps.

Before
you ask.

Does PROOF CHECK include a redesign?

No. It is a review and recommendation service. New design concepts, artwork corrections and implementation are scoped separately.

Can you review a whole range?

The fixed fee covers one product and its primary packaging. Multiple products, additional packaging formats or a wider brand review require a separately agreed scope before work begins.

Will the review confirm that my packaging is ready to print?

No. Production observations are high-level and based on the information supplied. This is not a detailed prepress audit, manufacturing approval, regulatory compliance assessment or physical performance test. Where further checks are needed, we will identify them.

Do I have to implement the recommendations with Proof?

No. Your report stands alone, and there is no obligation to commission further work. The project credit is available only when you proceed with Proof Creative on a qualifying project.

When will I receive the report?

We agree the delivery timeframe before you book, based on the scope, availability and receipt of the required materials.

Is PROOF CHECK a prerequisite for a new project?

No. Already have a clear brief for a new brand or packaging project? You can enquire directly. PROOF CHECK is an option, not a prerequisite.
