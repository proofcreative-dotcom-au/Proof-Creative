Brand identity

We develop the visual language of your business: the logo, typography, colour, graphic assets and iconography that make it recognisable. Clear usage guidance helps that identity hold together across its applications.

Starting point
Business context, audience, competitors, ambition and any existing assets.
What we deliver
A logo and identity system, typography and colour, core graphic assets and usage rules.
Scope

The identity provides the foundation for packaging, digital and campaign work. The applications are agreed as part of the project.

Ways to work together

Brand Foundation

The essential identity for a new business or direction: brand direction, logo, typography, colour and clear guidance for using them.

Brand System

A more developed identity, with key applications and detailed guidelines to support consistent use across the business.

Brand Refresh

A considered evolution of an existing identity, strengthening what needs attention while preserving what already works.

Digital & social

Website design

We shape the structure, user experience and visual design of your website, working with your developer to carry the identity through to the finished site.

Social strategy & campaign creative

We establish a clear direction for your channels, content and campaigns, with creative concepts and assets that feel connected to the wider brand. Specialist production support is brought in where needed.

Channel direction, profile optimisation, content themes, campaign concepts and ad creative.

Brand & Digital Partner
